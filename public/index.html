Tap four bolt holes as far apart as you can on one flat panel: top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right. This corrects perspective.

Count spaces, not holes: 9 holes is 8 gaps. 0.1524 m is 6 in. Measure your gym's spacing once; 6 in against 8 in is a 33% error on everything.
